# 2026-11-18 Why chip bags are full of "air" (evergreen)

## YouTube
Title: Why Is Your Chip Bag Half Air? It's Not a Scam #Shorts

Description:
That "air" in a chip bag is mostly nitrogen. Factories flush out oxygen, which makes the oil in chips go rancid, and the gas cushions the chips on the way to the store. The weight on the label is the chips, not the gas.

## Fact sheet
Checked (3+ sources): gas is mostly nitrogen, not ordinary air (NBC/Schwarcz, Sorbent Systems, Union Kitchen, PMC study); nitrogen displaces oxygen that makes chip oils rancid/stale (NBC, Sorbent, PMC study, Union Kitchen, nexAir); gas cushions chips against crushing, "pillow pack" (NBC, Sorbent, Union Kitchen, nexAir, Inox Air); flushing happens before sealing (Union Kitchen, Sorbent, Levapack).
Two sources: label weight is the chips (NBC/Schwarcz; net-weight labelling generally excludes packaging gas).
Not claimed: exact % of air in a bag; exact shelf life.
