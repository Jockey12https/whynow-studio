# 2026-11-17 How hackers heated up 700 supermarkets' fridges (news)

## YouTube
Title: Hackers Heated Up 700 Supermarkets' Fridges. Here's How #Shorts

Description:
In October, refrigeration controllers at about 700 French stores were hacked and switched into defrost, spoiling food. They were reachable from the internet, and France's cyber agency pointed to basic security failures. France blames an IRGC-linked hacking unit; Iran rejects the claim.
